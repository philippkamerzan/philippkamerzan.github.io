Geology Dash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Geology Dash has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. Game progress and settings (completed worlds, best progress, collected fossils, language, sound options) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Android and iOS apps)

The mobile apps show ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the world menu and a full-screen ad between runs after 15 completed attempts. If an ad is unavailable, the next run can still start. Ads never appear during a run. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interactions, as described in Google's policy: Google advertising privacy policy

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalised ads. On iOS the app does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

The web version of the game shows no ads and sends no data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the ads are not personalised for users identified as children.
